How it works in simple words

  1. Each step in a product's life is saved as a record (what, where, when, who).
  2. Every record stores a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the record before it, so the records are linked like a chain.
  3. The person who adds a record also signs it with a private key, so we know who did it.
  4. If anybody changes an old record, its fingerprint no longer matches and the check fails.
  5. Each role can only do its own job. A shop cannot create a new product, a customer cannot ship anything.
